ABOUT THE CLUB

Welcome to Social Impact

Social Impact is the Community Service Club of IDEAL Degree & PG College. The club provides students with opportunities to participate in meaningful community activities and contribute through teamwork and service.

From awareness campaigns and volunteering to environmental and social initiatives, students can work together to make a positive difference.

Support people and participate in service initiatives.
