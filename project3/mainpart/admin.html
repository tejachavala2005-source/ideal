Staff access

Staff accounts and permissions are managed in Supabase, not in this browser.

Provisioning staff

Create staff accounts in Supabase Authentication, then have a Supabase project administrator grant each account an ADMIN role or a COORDINATOR role assigned to one club in the staff_members table. Coordinators are limited to their assigned club by database row-level security.
