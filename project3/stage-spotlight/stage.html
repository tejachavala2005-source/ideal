ABOUT THE CLUB

Welcome to Stage & Spotlight

Stage & Spotlight is the Performing Arts Club of IDEAL Degree & PG College, created to provide students with a platform to discover and showcase their creative talents.

From music and dance to drama, anchoring and entertainment, students can participate, collaborate and perform on stage.

Singing, instruments and musical performances.
