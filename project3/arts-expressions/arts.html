ABOUT THE CLUB

Welcome to Art & Expression

Art & Expression is the Creative Arts Club of IDEAL Degree & PG College. It gives students a space to explore imagination, experiment with different art forms and express ideas through creativity.

From painting and sketching to design, craft and creative showcases, students can learn, collaborate and confidently share their work.

Creative Skills

Explore drawing, painting, design and other creative techniques.

Ideas & Innovation

Turn original ideas into visual concepts and creative projects.

Meet fellow students, collaborate and showcase your talent.
