ABOUT THE CLUB

Welcome to Voice & Vision

Voice & Vision is the Communication Club of IDEAL Degree & PG College. It creates a supportive space for students to improve public speaking, presentation, discussion and leadership communication.

Through debates, speeches, group discussions, storytelling and stage activities, students can develop confidence and learn to communicate ideas effectively.

Public Speaking

Build confidence and express your thoughts clearly before an audience.

Creative Thinking

Develop ideas, viewpoints and communication strategies through activities.

Team Communication

Learn collaboration, active listening and constructive discussion.
